How to Claim Gemini Student Offer

1

Go to Student Portal

Open the official Gemini for Students portal (gemini.google/students/) in your web browser.

2

Sign in with Personal Google Account

Log in using your personal Google Account.

3

Verify Student Status

Select the Verify Student Status option and enter your college/university details along with your official .edu or institution email ID.

4

Complete Setup

Submit your student verification; once approved, your account will be upgraded to 1 Year Free access with higher usage limits and storage.
